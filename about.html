About Auspicious Craft

Since 1996

A Heritage of Resin Craft & Devotional Art

For over thirty years, Auspicious Craft has crafted premium resin nativity sets, hand-painted statues and Christmas ornaments in our own 3,000 m² factory. What began as a small family workshop is today a modern direct factory exporting to wholesalers, distributors and retailers in more than 20 European countries.

Every piece combines time-honoured sculpting with precision casting, fine hand-painting and rigorous quality control — so the scenes you sell arrive exactly as your customers imagine them. We welcome OEM and ODM partnerships, custom colourways and private-label packaging.

Our Craft Process

1. Resin Sculpting & Casting

Original sculptures are recreated in fine eco-friendly resin using precision moulds for consistent detail at scale.

Artisans apply layered colour, gilding and UV-protective finishes by hand — piece by piece.

3. Quality Inspection

Every batch passes a multi-point QC check for detail, colour, weight and export-safe packaging.
